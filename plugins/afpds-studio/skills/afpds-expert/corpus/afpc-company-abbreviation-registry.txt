Notes: 
 

IPDS printers and transforms place the company abbreviation in the manufacturer field of the 
XOH-OPC reply; this value must be three EBCDIC characters.  Company abbreviations are 
also used in the CMR name for device-dependent CMRs. 

 

IANA is the Internet Assigned Number Authority.
