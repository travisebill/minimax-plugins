## 1. YPP 資格門檻

### 1.1 現行（完整 YPP，廣告收益）

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851>

> "Get **1,000 subscribers** with **4,000 qualified watch hours** in the last 12 months, or
> Get 1,000 subscribers with **10 million qualified Shorts views** in the last 90 days."

### 1.2 🔴 2027-02-01 門檻翻倍

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009>

> "Starting **Feb. 1, 2027**, YPP entry thresholds for new creators are changing to
> **8,000 qualified watch hours** in the last 365 days, or **20M qualified Shorts views**
> in the last 90 days, in addition to still needing 1k subscribers. If you are already in YPP,
> your status is not impacted by this update.
>
> There are **no changes** to the eligibility requirements for fan funding, YouTube Creator
> Partnerships, or YouTube Shopping. These products will continue to help creators to start their
> earning journeys even earlier when they reach **500 subscribers and either 3,000 qualified
> watch hours** in the last year or 3M qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days."

**台灣在適用名單內。**

| 層級 | 門檻 | 適用 |
|---|---|---|
| **fan funding** | **500 訂閱 + 3,000 h** | **近期務實目標，不受 2027 變更影響** |
| 廣告（現況） | 1,000 訂閱 + 4,000 h | |
| 廣告（2027-02-01 起） | 1,000 訂閱 + **8,000 h** | 籌備期約 4 個月 |

### 1.3 兩個容易踩的坑（官方明文）

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851>

> "**Keep in mind that any qualified watch hours from Shorts views in the Shorts Feed won't
> count towards the 4,000 qualified public watch hours threshold.**"

→ **Shorts Feed 的觀看不計入門檻。**

**未轉為 VOD 的直播不計入。**（已驗證於官方門檻頁的合格觀看小時定義）

→ **只能用長片 VOD 累積觀看小時。24/7 直播無效。**

### 1.4 收益分成

官方：**Watch Time Ads 55%**、**Shorts & Feed Ads 45%**。

## 2. 🔴 Inauthentic / Generic Content（變現紅線）

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392>

### 2.1 審核者檢查什麼

> "Our reviewers **will check your channel and content** against our policies.
> **Since they can't check every video**, our reviewers may focus on your channel's:
> - Main theme
> - Most viewed videos
> - Newest videos
> - Biggest proportion of watch time
> - **Video metadata (including titles, thumbnails, and descriptions)**
> - **Channel's "About" section**"

**🔴 對 15 支的冷啟動頻道：等於每支都可能會被看到。**
這是「標題與說明策略」存在的根本原因。

### 2.2 Generic or Repetitive Content

> "Generic or repetitive content includes content that looks like it's **made with a template**,
> or that may feel **repetitive to viewers after watching several videos in a row from the same
> channel**. […] channels where content feels **interchangeable from video to video are not
> allowed to monetize**."

**禁止清單**（與本頻道直接相關）：

> "**AI-generated content made with generic or unoriginal templates giving the impression of mass
> production without adding the creator's original, authentic insights or perspective**"

> "Collections of songs from different artists (**even if you have their permission**)"

> "with minimal or no narrative, commentary, or educational value"

**允許清單**：

> "**Similar content, like a series following a set of characters across episodes** […]
> but in which each video has a **distinct storyline, focus, or concept**"

> "**Same intro and outro** for your videos, but the bulk of your content is different"

> "using AI to **visualize a unique character and narrative you invented**"

> "**generate a unique background visual for your content**"

> "using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline, script, thumbnail, title, or infographic"

**三個可直接引用的結論**：

1. 「三國人物系列」本身**是允許的**（官方明文允許 series following a set of characters）——
   前提是 **each video has a distinct storyline, focus, or concept**。
2. **統一片頭片尾是允許的**，不要為避嫌破壞美學一致性。
3. 政策禁止的尾巴是「**沒有**創作者原創見解」——所以必須讓創作決策**可見**（見 SKILL.md R5）。

### 2.3 Spam Policy

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973>

> "Channels that use the **exact same background music and repetitive AI generated imagery**
> across many videos"

→ **視覺必須每支專屬，且由該支音樂的實際內容驅動生成。**
若循環動畫與音樂段落結構無對應關係，這條就是刀。

## 3. 🔴 AI 揭露（強制）

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491>

### 3.1 「必須揭露」清單第一項

> "Examples of content creators need to disclose
> To help keep viewers informed about the content they're viewing, we require creators to
> disclose content that is generated or meaningfully altered with AI when it appears realistic.
> Examples of content, edits, or video assistance that creators need to disclose:
> **AI generated music**          ← 純器樂音樂，**不論視覺是否寫實**
> AI generated extra footage of a real place […]
> AI generated realistic videos of a match between two real professional tennis players
> […]"

**🔴 這是最常被搞錯的一點。** 社群流傳的「純音樂或抽象動畫不用揭露」**與官方條文不符**。
豁免條件是「**不寫實（not realistic）**」或「**輕微編輯（minor）」，
但純器樂音樂被**直接列為需要揭露**，不在豁免清單。

### 3.2 官方明文：揭露無成本

> "**Disclosing AI content won't limit a video's audience or impact its eligibility to earn money.**"

**→ 上傳時 Attributes → "AI use" 勾 Yes。**
不揭露的處罰包含移除內容或 YPP 停權。**永遠不要為了躲標籤而不揭露。**

### 3.3 不需揭露的例子（避免誤判）

> "Someone riding a unicorn through a fantastical world"
> "Using an AI-generated or altered animation of a missile in a fully animated video"
> "Production assistance, like using generative AI tools to create or improve a video outline,
>   script, thumbnail, title, or infographic"
> "Caption creation" / "Video sharpening, upscaling or repair" / "Idea generation"
> "Gameplay footage from video games"

## 4. hashtag 政策

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658>

### 4.1 官方從未聲稱 hashtag 提升排名

> "Hashtags allow you to easily **connect your content with other videos or playlists** which
> share the same hashtag on YouTube and YouTube Music."

**全文沒有任何一句說 hashtag 會提升搜尋排名或曝光。**

排名訊號在 <https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805>：

> "**How well the title, description, and video content match the viewer's search.**"

### 4.2 Over-tagging

> "**Don't add too many tags** in a single video or playlist. **The more tags you add,
> the less relevant they become** for viewers or listeners who are searching.
> If a video or playlist has **more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag** on that content."

### 4.3 Non-hashtags（易忽略）

> "While adding hashtags is allowed, **adding ordinary descriptive tags or repetitive sentences
> in the description is (still) prohibited.** If you violate this policy, your video or playlist
> may be removed or penalized."

→ **堆疊描述性標籤字串本身違反政策。**

### 4.4 只顯示 3 個

> "Out of all the hashtags you add to the video's description, **up to three hashtags** that are
> considered most engaging will appear by your video title."

## 5. 排名與改標題

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805>

### 5.1 🔴 改標題本身不會重新排名

> "**Does changing a video's title or thumbnail re-rank the video in the algorithm?
> Maybe**, but it's because our systems are responding to how viewers are interacting with your
> video differently **rather than the act of changing the video title or thumbnail.**
> When your video looks different to viewers, it changes how viewers interact with it when it's
> offered to them. Changing your video title and thumbnail can be an effective way to get more
> views, but **don't change what is working.**"

**→ 改舊片標題救不回已離開推薦池的影片。**
但官方**確實**說改標題「can be an effective way to get more views」——
前提是**觀眾仍然看得到**。已發布 1～5 週、32～451 觀看的影片已離開推薦池。

### 5.2 長片在 watch time 上占優

> "Our discovery system uses **absolute and relative watch time** as signals when deciding
> audience engagement and we encourage you to do the same. Broadly speaking, relative watch time
> is more important for short videos and **absolute watch time is more important for longer videos**."

**→ 這是長時長音樂的核心優勢。策略本身是對的，問題在關鍵字覆蓋。**

### 5.3 新頻道需要內容臨界量

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559651>

> "For new channels, gaining this credibility and building a community takes time.
> **A key factor here is developing a critical mass of content.**"

> "**Avoid filler and resist artificially stretching content**, as audiences generally prefer
> concise, high-value information."

## 6. mid-roll 廣告

來源：<https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6175006>

> "On monetized videos that are **8 minutes or longer**, you can turn on ads during the middle
> of the video (known as 'mid-rolls')."

> "**Ad slots placed at natural breakpoints, such as a pause in audio or transition in visual,
> are more likely to serve ads as our systems generally find higher viewer retention at these
> breakpoints.** Mid-roll ad slots that can be disruptive to the viewer experience, such as
> mid-sentence or mid-action are less likely to serve ads."

**兩個實務結論**：

1. **60～120 分鐘的片可被插 9～15 支廣告**，且官方專挑「音樂停頓 / 視覺轉場」處插。
2. **反直覺：留存高的地方更容易被插廣告**。章節切點同時是廣告切點。

### 6.1 「無廣告版」標題現象的解釋

中文專注音樂前 10 名中幾乎每支都寫「無廣告版」／「100% 無廣告」／「【No Ads】」
（實測見 `title-keyword-strategy.md` §4.1）。**這是對抗 mid-roll 的信任訊號。**

注意：「無廣告版」是**無法兌現的承諾**，只能拉高點擊率。
真要無廣告只能靠 Premium 觀看或站外消費。**不要在說明中承諾自己做不到的事。**

## 7. 官方承認的類別數據

來源：<https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/ambient-music/>（2025-12-05）

> "It has evolved into a broad spectrum '**ambience channels**' that blend music and visuals,
> specifically designed to help serve needs such as **sleep, relaxation, and focus**.
> These channels **prioritize endless streams and long runtimes with looped visuals, perfect for
> background use.** […] the YouTube channel **Soothing Relaxation has received more than 4.8B
> channel views**."（Source: YouTube data, Global, Lifetime.）

來源：<https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/study-with-me/>（2024-02-05）

> "There were **more than 520M views of videos with 'study with me' in the title in 2023**"
> （Source: YouTube Data, Global, Jan 1 – Dec 31, 2023）

**兩個重要更正**：

1. **「YouTube 降權循環／靜態畫面」找不到官方依據。**
   官方反而把「endless streams + long runtimes + looped visuals」描述為**正常形態**。
   15 秒循環影片**形式上完全符合官方描述**。真正的刀是 §2.2 與 §2.3，不是畫面本身。
2. **這是主流類別，不是小眾。** 問題從來不是「有沒有需求」，
   而是頻道沒有進入推薦所需的觀看樣本。

## 8. 搜尋匹配的操作建議（由官方條文推導）

官方說明搜尋匹配靠 title + description + 影片內容（§4.1）。推導出的實務做法：

- **說明最前面放純陳述式搜尋匹配句**（給機器讀，不文學包裝）
- **英文為主中文為副**（實測中文標題在英文關鍵字無 10 萬級案例）
- **片名帶時長**（受眾搜 `1 hour`／`3 hours`）
- **帶樂器名**（實測這是量級最高的關鍵字類別）
- **帶明確用途詞**（focus / study / deep work / writing / sleep / reading）
- **不可用歷史人物/事件當主關鍵字**（`three kingdoms music` 已被原聲內容佔滿）
