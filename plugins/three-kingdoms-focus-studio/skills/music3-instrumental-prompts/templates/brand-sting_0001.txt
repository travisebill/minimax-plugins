Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 120. key is C, and scale is major. Modern Cinematic Pop / Brand Sting.
Global Emotional Progression: Immediate and confident. Impact within the first beat, a clean and
fast arc, and a fully resolved landing within seconds. There is no slow build and no long decay.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: A 10–15 second advertising logo sting, a product reveal, a title
card, the moment a logo hits screen.
Sonics & Production Profile: Glossy and wide with a bright, polished master and a tight controlled
low end. Loudness-forward and confident, but never harsh or compressed into mush.

Vocal Details
Lead Instrument: Instrumental — no vocals, no vocal chops, no choir, no spoken word. The lead melodic
role is carried by a bright plucked synth motif, doubled an octave up by a bell-like FM synth.
Performance: Short, tight, fully articulated plucks with a fast attack and a clean, controlled
decay. No reverb tail on the lead — the motif must read as precise and engineered, not washed.
Harmonic Bed: A deep controlled sub-bass supports the motif, and a single sustained low-string swell
appears for one bar to create lift.
Spatial: Tight and centred in the low end, wide and bright above. No ambient wash, no hall, no room.

Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering): The track opens on a single
hard-synced percussive hit, then a tight four-on-the-floor pattern locks in immediately. The plucked
motif enters on the following bar. The low-string swell lifts for exactly one bar, a single wide
impact hit marks the brand moment, and everything resolves on a clean final cadence. The track ends
on the resolution with a short natural decay — no fade, no tail.
Groove & Foundation Progression: Four-on-the-floor throughout, straight and confident, with a deep
controlled sub-bass on the downbeat. The low end is tight and modern, not 808-boomy, and it never
drowns the midrange. No half-time drop, no break, no breakdown.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: None. No risers, no reverse cymbals, no build beyond three
seconds, no ambient texture, no vinyl noise. The discipline here is the point — every sound must earn
its place within fifteen seconds, and anything decorative is a liability.
