Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 84. key is D, and scale is minor. Cinematic Orchestral / Epic Score.
Global Emotional Progression: Cold isolation, then slow accumulating tension, then an explosive
heroic release, then a hollow restrained aftermath. The climax must feel earned, never announced.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: Game boss-reveal trailer, historical war-drama montage, a last stand
on a collapsing ridge line.
Sonics & Production Profile: Wide dark soundstage, deep sub-bass weight, shimmering high string
textures. Dynamic range travels from near-silence to dense wall-of-sound, then collapses back to
almost nothing. Long hall reverb tail, no hard digital edges.

Vocal Details
Lead Instrument: Instrumental — no vocals, no choir, no spoken word, no vocal samples. The lead
melodic role is carried by a solo erhu with audible pitch slides and breathy bowing through the first
half, later doubled by a legato violin line.
Performance: Rubato and unaccompanied at the opening, the erhu stating its motif alone. The violin
enters legato and without vibrato, holding long sustained notes. Vibrato is withheld until the
climax, and then only on the highest line.
Harmonic Bed: Low cello and viola sustain a pedal drone underneath. A grand piano places sparse,
reverberant chords in the gaps rather than running a continuous pattern.
Spatial: Long hall reverb on all melodic material, with a short darker reflection under the
percussive layer for contrast.

Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering): Opens on the solo erhu motif with no
percussion. A low cello drone enters beneath it, then sparse frame-drum pulses establish a slow
marching tempo. Violas add a rising countermelody as tension thickens. Low brass swells and a timpani
roll mark the pre-chorus. The climax lands with the full string section, taiko-driven low end, a
brass fanfare, and a distorted electric guitar layer cutting through for aggressive texture. A beat
of near-silence precedes the final rise. The outro strips to a single sustained string harmonic that
decays into silence.
Groove & Foundation Progression: The first section has no percussion at all — the groove is entirely
the erhu's phrasing. The frame-drum pulse enters at roughly half the final tempo and sits behind the
beat rather than on it, which keeps the opening feeling weighted rather than marching. In the climax
the pattern expands with snare rolls, cymbal crashes, and driving kick, pushing the low end to the
front of the mix.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Cinematic risers and reverse cymbal swells mark each
transition and never appear twice in the same form. Sub-bass drops accent only the heaviest drum
hits. Ambient wind texture fills the quiet sections and sells the sense of scale.
