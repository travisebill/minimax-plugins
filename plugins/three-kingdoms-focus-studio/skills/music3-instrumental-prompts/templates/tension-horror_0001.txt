Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 68. key is E, and scale is minor. Dark Underscore / Tense Minimal.
Global Emotional Progression: Unease that tightens almost imperceptibly. The listener should realise
they are already afraid several bars before anything visibly happens. The ending leaves the question
unanswered rather than resolving it.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: Horror underscore, a slow approach, a scene where something is
present but off-screen, a thriller sequence that is holding its breath.
Sonics & Production Profile: Dark, dry, and narrow. Short decays, almost no reverb, and a
high-mid-heavy frequency profile that makes the quiet parts feel exposed rather than comforting.
Silence is used as an instrument.

Vocal Details
Lead Instrument: Instrumental — no vocals, no choir, no whispers, no spoken word, no vocal samples.
The lead melodic role is carried by a detuned upright piano, its natural wobble left audible, plus a
bowed cello that enters only near the end.
Performance: The piano repeats a short, unresolved figure — the same five notes, never resolving —
with a slightly uneven human touch and a percussive mechanism noise audible between notes. The cello
bowing is extremely slow and quiet, more texture than melody.
Harmonic Bed: A low sustained drone a semitone below the piano's root holds continuously and never
moves. No other sustained element.
Spatial: Very dry and close, with almost no reverb. The lack of space is deliberate and is what makes
the track feel claustrophobic.

Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering): A low drone enters alone for the first
eight bars. The upright piano figure enters, thin and exposed, and repeats without variation for the
bulk of the track. A slow metallic scrape appears once around the midpoint and never returns. Near
the end a bowed cello joins, barely audible at first, rising just enough to be noticed. The piano
stops abruptly mid-figure — not a resolution, just a stop — and the drone continues alone into the
fade.
Groove & Foundation Progression: There is no groove. No drums, no percussion, no repeating rhythmic
figure. Time is held by the piano's uneven repetition, which is precisely what makes it unsettling.
Nothing is quantized and nothing locks.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Very sparse and irregular. A single high metallic ping appears
exactly once. No risers, no impacts, no big build, no cymbal swell, no sub-bass drop. Any
conventional tension-building device would defeat the purpose — the tension must come from
withholding, not from escalation.
